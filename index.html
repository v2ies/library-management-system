Library Rules & Regulations

Before requesting , please review and agree to the following:

  • Do not write, highlight, or mark on any page.
  • Do not fold, tear, or bend the pages or cover.
  • Keep the book away from food, drinks, and moisture.
  • Return the book on or before the due date on your slip.
  • Report any existing damage to the librarian before borrowing.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for.
Overdue books are sanctioned ₱10.00 for every day late.
